# 🛍️ Google Play listing

## 📄 App name

Document Viewer

## 📝 Short description

A simple, private file viewer. No ads, no account, no data collection.

## 📖 Full description

A calm space for your documents.

Document Viewer helps you read PDFs and simplified Word, PowerPoint, and Excel text previews on your Android device. Open a document, pick up where you left off, and keep your reading focused with a dark, emerald-accented interface.

📖 READ AT YOUR OWN PACE

• Scroll continuously through your document.
• Pinch or double-tap to zoom in on details.
• Jump straight to a page.
• Return to your last page with automatic reading progress and a continue-reading card.
• Reopen documents from your recent-files library.

✍️ MAKE YOUR MARK

• Draw directly on a page.
• Tap to place typed text.
• Undo marks as you work.
• Use two fingers to move or zoom while drawing.

💾 SAVE A SEPARATE COPY

Export your drawings and added text in a marked-up PDF. Your original file stays unchanged. Save your copy before leaving the document to keep your marks.

🔒 PRIVATE BY DESIGN

No ads. No accounts. No data collection. Read and annotate local documents offline, without granting the app internet access. Choose the files you want to open using Android's file picker.

📂 OPEN DOCUMENTS YOUR WAY

Open PDF, Word (.doc/.docx), PowerPoint (.ppt/.pptx), and Excel (.xls/.xlsx) files from within the app or from Files or another compatible app. Android lets you select it as your default PDF viewer when the option is available.

ℹ️ IMPORTANT DETAILS

• Office files use simplified text previews, not original layouts. Images, charts, embedded objects, and animations are not shown. Legacy presentations may have different text order. Spreadsheet values may be unformatted, and formulas are not recalculated.
• Office previews are limited to 32 MiB input and 400 generated pages; additional content limits apply. Encrypted Office files are not supported. Macros never run and external content is never fetched.
• Saving an Office preview creates a PDF, not an edited Word, PowerPoint, or Excel file. Sharing the original keeps its Office format unchanged.
• Exported copies are flattened into page images. Searchable text, links, forms, and accessibility tags are not preserved in the copy.
• Password-protected, damaged, or unavailable PDFs may not open.
• Requires Android 7.0 or later.
